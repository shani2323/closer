    Truth or Dare

    After 3 truths in a row, the next pick must be a dare. Skip a dare and you get a penalty. The game waits until your partner confirms it arrived. You can always choose another penalty. A dare for losing Tower or Ludo earns no point: your partner judges it, and a dare not done properly counts as a lie, which takes a point away.

    Our Tower

    0 floors

    Take turns dropping boxes from the swinging crane. You each have 3 lives. The first box hangs still; the swing gets wider as the tower grows. Every floor brings 25 people, and each perfect drop in a row adds 25 more. Every 10 floors is a goal worth 100 people. Crooked drops make the tower lean and sway; perfect drops straighten it. A box that misses costs you a life, and so does a bad drop when the tower leans too far. The tower never falls and passes to the other one. Lose all 3 lives and you get a dare, then a new tower starts. Drag the tower to look up and down.

    Ludo

    How to play

    Two pieces each, a queen and a rook. Each waits in your base until you roll a 6 for it, only the first time: after that any number brings it out again. Throw the die across the board with your finger, shake your phone, or tap Roll. Then tap a glowing piece. There are three 🚀 and two 💀. A 🚀 jumps you 3 ahead, then flies to a new random square. Land on a 💀 and that piece goes back to base. The rockets and the skulls jump to new random squares at random moments: never next to each other, never under a piece, never on the paths to the heart. Land on the other's piece and you flick yours into it (pull back, let go: harder pull, harder hit) to send it back to base; stars and start squares are safe. A 6 or a catch rolls again. The die is fair but helpful: a number that catches a piece within 6 squares, or a 6 for a piece's first start, comes up a little more often. Every square counts, stars and starts too. Go once round the ring (the arrows show the way), then turn into your own coloured path to the heart. That path stays locked 🔒 until you catch one of the other's pieces, or a 💀 hits you twice. Until then your pieces wait in front of it, unless that roll catches a piece just past it: a number too big for every piece passes the turn. If all the other's pieces are already on their heart path while yours is still locked, they win. Bring both pieces onto the heart with an exact roll to win. The loser gets a dare, then a new game starts.
